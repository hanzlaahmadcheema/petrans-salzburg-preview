Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What happens if my flight to Salzburg is delayed?

We track your flight number in real-time. If your arrival is delayed, your chauffeur automatically adjusts the pickup schedule. Your 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time starts only after your plane touches down on the runway.

Are child seats and ski equipment really free of charge?

Yes, 100% free! We carry certified infant seats, child booster cushions, and ski/snowboard bags at zero additional fee. Just select the required quantities in Step 4 of the booking form so our driver can configure the vehicle in advance.

Can I pay the driver in cash or by credit card upon arrival?

Yes. You can choose to pay the driver directly upon arrival at your destination. Our chauffeurs carry mobile POS terminals accepting all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay) or cash in Euros. You can also choose to prepay online.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer 100% free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. If your plans change, simply notify us by WhatsApp (+43 660 6416464) or email.

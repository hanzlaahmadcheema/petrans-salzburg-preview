Tour Highlights & Sights

Key landmarks included along your private scenic route.

Kehlsteinhaus Summit (1,834m)

Breathtaking 360° panoramic views across Bavaria and the Salzburg Alps.

Historic Brass Elevator

The original 1938 brass-clad elevator shaft boring 124m through Mount Kehlstein.

Lake Königssee (Emerald Fjord)

Glacial lake enclosed by Mount Watzmann; stroll to the painter's corner (Malerwinkel).

Berchtesgaden Market Square

Centuries-old Bavarian village with frescoed townhouses and artisan woodcarvers.

Winter Weather Contingency

November–April alternative: Rossfeld Panorama Road & Berchtesgaden 500-year Salt Mine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we adjust the departure time or customized itinerary?
Yes, completely. Because this is a 100% private tour, you dictate pickup time, photo stops, and duration at each village. Just coordinate with your chauffeur.
What if the weather is rainy or foggy on our tour day?
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours prior to departure. Our chauffeurs also know alternative scenic routes and indoor attractions.
How and when do we pay?
No advance deposit is required online. You can pay your chauffeur upon tour completion via cash, debit, or major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX).

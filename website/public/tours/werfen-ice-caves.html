Tour Highlights & Experience

A balanced day combining alpine geology, cable car ascents, and medieval Austrian history.

Eisriesenwelt Ice Cave Wonder

Ascend via Austria's steepest cable car to the cave entrance at 1,641m elevation. Walk through vast subterranean cathedrals carved by ice formations, illuminated only by traditional magnesium lamps carried by visitors.

900-Year Hohenwerfen Castle

Visit the historic 11th-century fortress featured in Hollywood classics (Where Eagles Dare). Experience the historic State Falconry Center with daily bird of prey flight demonstrations over the alpine valley.
